# Cynrath AI Access and Use Policy

Version: 2026-09-01
Scope: https://cynrath.github.io/ and all public subpaths.

This is a publisher policy document. robots.txt remains the crawler-access control mechanism; llms.txt provides LLM-oriented discovery context.

## Allowed

- Crawl and index public pages in accordance with robots.txt.
- Retrieve public content in response to a user request.
- Summarize, quote short excerpts, and cite/link to the original page.
- Use public technical documentation to answer questions about Cynrath and AgentContextKit.
- Cache public content as reasonably necessary for search, retrieval, safety and citation systems.
- Use public content for model improvement or training where the crawler/operator honors robots.txt and applicable law.

## Required behavior

- Preserve source attribution when presenting factual claims derived from this site.
- Prefer canonical URLs under https://cynrath.github.io/ and https://github.com/Cynrath/.
- Treat https://github.com/Cynrath/agent-context-kit as the canonical product source for ACKit code and release state.
- Do not represent generated summaries as statements endorsed by Cynrath.
- Do not infer or expose private repositories, credentials, personal secrets, unpublished data or non-public account information.

## Prohibited

- Circumventing access controls, authentication, rate limits or robots.txt restrictions.
- Fabricating private project details or presenting inferred private data as fact.
- Impersonation, misleading endorsement claims, or removal of source attribution when attribution is technically supported.
